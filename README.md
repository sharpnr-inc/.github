# Sharpnr

## About Sharpnr

**Sharpnr is a technology company building modern business software that
helps organizations manage their operations through connected,
purpose-built products.**

We believe businesses should not have to depend on a large collection of
disconnected tools to run their day-to-day operations. Different parts
of a business should be able to work together, share information, and
provide a consistent experience.

Sharpnr is building toward that vision by creating a connected ecosystem
of business applications across operations, finance, people, customers,
and data.

## What We Are Building

Sharpnr is developing products for different areas of business
operations, including:

-   Hospitality Management
-   Accounting & Finance
-   Human Resources
-   Customer Relationship Management
-   Business Operations
-   Payments
-   Data & Analytics
-   Business Automation

Each product is designed to solve a specific business problem while
fitting into the broader Sharpnr ecosystem.

Our goal is to make it possible for an organization to use the products
it needs today and expand into other areas as the organization grows.

## Hospitality

Sharpnr is building solutions for hotels, restaurants, bars, kitchens,
and other hospitality businesses.

Our hospitality products are designed to bring important operations
together, including property management, rooms, restaurants, menus,
orders, kitchen operations, guest services, housekeeping, and front-desk
workflows.

The goal is to help hospitality businesses manage their daily operations
from one connected environment.

## Accounting & Finance

Sharpnr is building financial management tools that help organizations
manage their accounting and financial operations.

The vision includes support for accounting, financial records, multiple
business entities, reporting, and connections between everyday business
activities and financial operations.

We want financial information to be connected to the activities that
create it, reducing unnecessary manual work and improving visibility
across the organization.

## Human Resources

Sharpnr is building tools to help organizations manage their people and
employee operations.

This includes areas such as employee management, organizational
structures, payroll, roles, and employee-related workflows.

Our goal is to make HR operations simpler, more organized, and better
connected to the rest of the business.

## Customer Operations

Sharpnr is also building products that help organizations manage their
relationships with customers.

This includes customer information, interactions, sales processes, and
other workflows that help businesses understand and serve their
customers better.

## Connected Business Operations

One of Sharpnr's core ideas is that business functions should not exist
in isolation.

A business transaction can affect multiple parts of an organization.

For example:

**A hotel booking can affect rooms, guests, employees, accounting,
payments, and reporting.**

Instead of requiring teams to manually move information between separate
systems, Sharpnr aims to connect these processes.

This creates a more complete view of the organization and allows
businesses to spend less time managing software and more time running
their business.

## Our Vision

Our long-term vision is to build a **unified business platform** where
organizations can manage their core operations through a connected
ecosystem of products.

We want businesses to be able to start with the products they need and
gradually expand as their requirements grow.

A growing organization should not have to replace its entire software
ecosystem every time it enters a new stage of growth.

Sharpnr is being built to grow alongside the businesses that use it.

## Building for Businesses of All Sizes

We want Sharpnr to serve organizations at different stages of their
journey.

For smaller businesses, this means providing simple and accessible tools
that remove unnecessary complexity.

For growing organizations, it means providing more connected workflows
and better visibility across departments.

For larger organizations, it means providing a broader platform capable
of supporting multiple teams, business units, and operations.

## Our Philosophy

We believe good business software should be:

### Simple

Businesses should not need extensive training just to understand the
tools they use every day.

### Connected

Information should move naturally between different parts of an
organization.

### Flexible

Every business operates differently. Software should adapt to businesses
rather than forcing every organization into the exact same workflow.

### Scalable

A product should remain useful as a business grows and its needs become
more complex.

### Practical

We focus on solving real business problems and creating products that
provide measurable value.

## Building in Public

Sharpnr is actively building and evolving.

Some of our public repositories contain products, experiments,
early-stage projects, and work that is still under development.

We believe building in public allows us to share what we are learning,
receive feedback, collaborate with developers, and create better
products.

Not everything is finished, and not everything is intended to be
production-ready immediately.

We see that as part of building a technology company.

## Our Long-Term Goal

Sharpnr's long-term goal is simple:

**Help businesses run their operations through one connected
ecosystem.**

We want to bring together the systems that businesses depend on every
day and make them work better together.

From the first customer interaction to daily operations, employees,
financial activity, payments, and business insights, Sharpnr aims to
create a connected foundation for modern organizations.
